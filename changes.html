    0.1.0 (ci-build)

    • First L2 and L3 content: generic personas and system actors as ActorDefinitions, user scenarios, and the business processes J1 to J4.1, generated from the harness sources.
    • The journeys tell the VaxLink story (issue #21): Somsack SENGMANY, born in Thailand (XXR), living in Lao PDR (XXH), seen again in Thailand a year later. Personas, system actors and stories recast; the business processes made generic (no person names in them).
    • New process steps: J2 adds the newborn to the household and saves the doses as reported, not verified; J3 adds the national schedule code beside the foreign vaccine code (the nurse decides unmapped products); J4.0 records the holder’s consent with a passcode and an expiry; J4.1 adds the home portal’s lane, which checks the passcode, returns the manifest and logs every access.
    • New examples: household Group, registration Questionnaire and response, reported and verified Immunizations, verification and adjudication Provenance, ConceptMap from product to schedule code, an illustrative schedule PlanDefinition, ImmunizationRecommendation and reminder Task, the four Lao doses, a six-dose IPS, Consent, and two AuditEvents (access granted, wrong passcode). Two illustrative code systems stand in for PreQual product ids and national schedule codes.
    • New page: VaxLink examples, mapping each walkthrough screen and payload to its example here.
