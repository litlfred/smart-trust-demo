    The journeys take place in two fictional countries, so that no real country, ministry or signing key is implied. Each is defined once, in scenarios/countries/, with a code from the ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 user-assigned range (XAA to XZZ), which never names a real country. Other journeys, such as the pilgrimage, reuse the same two countries and codes.

    Country Code Role Places In these journeys
    Onesa (adjective: Onesan) XXK home Lomaru (capital), Village 04, Tanavu (coastal town) Country B: the family’s home (J2, J3, J4.0), whose national portal serves their records abroad (J4.1); the pilgrim’s home in the pilgrimage
    Taifafeki (adjective: Taifafeki) XXR visited Lumara (capital), Kesaro (border province), Nalemi (tent valley), Toreva (plain) Country A: where the newborn is born (J1) and later seen at the point of care (J4.1); the host of the pilgrimage

    Codes

    Both codes are in the SMART Trust value set of GDHCN DEV participants, Participants-DEV, whose code system names XXK “Onesa” and XXR “Taifafeki”. Both are onboarded test participants of the GDHCN DEV network (WorldHealthOrganization/tng-participants-dev): XXR’s document signer key signs the birth-dose DVCs of J1, and XXK plays Onesa’s national portal in J4. Their keys are test keys, not any ministry’s.

    Reuse

    A journey that needs a home country and a visited country takes Onesa and Taifafeki from scenarios/countries/onesa.json and scenarios/countries/taifafeki.json: the name, adjective, code, role and places. The people in a journey are its own, and fictional too.
