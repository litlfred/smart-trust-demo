    The journeys do not restate requirements. Each step of a user scenario names the requirement it exercises by its id (for example STT-VER-011, read without verifying, in J2). Those requirements are defined, with their tests, on smart-trust-tools’ requirements page (sources in skills/requirements), which traces them to WHO SMART Trust and IHE ITI VHL.

    The WHO SMART Trust PHW requirements map onto the journeys as follows:

    • provide-a-vhl-to-a-receiver (Holder): J2, J3 and J4.1.
    • receive-a-vhl (Receiver): J2, J3 and J4.1.
    • publish-, receive-, distribute- and retrieve-pki-material: the trust anchor and the participants, preconditions of J1, J3, J4.0 and J4.1.
