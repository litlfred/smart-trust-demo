# wallet-hold-vhl

Hold one VHL QR per family member on a shared phone and show the one asked for (journeys J4.0, J4.1).

## What it does

The HC1 text of each member's HCERT, labelled by member; the phone never holds the passcode or the decryption key beyond what the link carries, and shows a member's QR only on request.
