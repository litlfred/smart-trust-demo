    What makes it work

    The phone reads the card offline, and keeps “what the family told us” clearly separate from “what we have verified”.

    Ask the room

    Is an “unverified but recorded” vaccine useful to the next clinician — or a risk?

    What if…

    What if the CHW phone could verify signatures offline too?

    Technically possible with a cached trust list — but who keeps it current on thousands of village phones?

    Policy takeaway

    Keep a clear line between what families report and what has been verified, and don’t grant trust too early.

    Policy questions for the table

    1. M3Does your national core data set tell a dose the family reported from one that was verified? Who defines that element?
    2. M4Who is allowed to verify a foreign credential: a health worker’s phone, a clinic, or only a national service?
