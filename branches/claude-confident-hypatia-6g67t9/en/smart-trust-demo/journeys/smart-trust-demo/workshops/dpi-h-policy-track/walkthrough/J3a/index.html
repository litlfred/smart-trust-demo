    What makes it work

    Thailand’s signing key is on the shared WHO trust list, so any clinic can check it locally.

    Ask the room

    When verification fails, what should the nurse do — and how is it explained to the family?

    What if…

    The card has been tampered with.

    The signature check fails, the doses stay unverified and the nurse follows the local catch-up policy.

    Policy takeaway

    Trust is decentralized: a Lao clinic verifies a Thai signature itself, without contacting Thailand.

    Policy questions for the table

    1. M1What must be true for this nurse to trust a record she did not create?
    2. M4Who would hold your national trust anchor, and who accepts a foreign signature on behalf of your health sector?
    3. M4What must your country have, and what must it decide, before it can verify a neighbour’s card offline? Name it; do not decide it here.
