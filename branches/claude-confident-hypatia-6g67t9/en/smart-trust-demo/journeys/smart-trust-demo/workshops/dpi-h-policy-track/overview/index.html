    The story in one sentence

    Somsack is born in Thailand, grows up in Lao PDR, and one year later a Thai doctor can see every vaccine he has ever had — without a single record being copied across the border.

    A baby and five people around him, four moments, twelve months. Follow the people first; the technology is what makes each hand-off possible.

    Four moments that matter

    1Thailand Born abroad birth day · Thailand A Lao baby gets his first vaccines in Thailand and leaves with signed paper cards. DAKS 2Lao PDR Found at home 3 weeks later · Lao PDR A health worker registers him offline and records the cards as "reported, not yet verified". KSVIBS 3Lao PDR Made official 4 weeks later · Vientiane District Health Center A nurse verifies the cards are genuine and fits the Thai doses into the Lao schedule. KSCH 4Cross-Border One family, one phone, a history abroad 6 to 12 months later · Lao PDR and Chiang Rai The family's records go on one shared phone as Verified Health Links (J4.0), and a year later a doctor back in Thailand reads the whole history from Lao PDR (J4.1). One chapter of the walkthrough, two BPMN processes, so two sub-journeys. DAKSBS
