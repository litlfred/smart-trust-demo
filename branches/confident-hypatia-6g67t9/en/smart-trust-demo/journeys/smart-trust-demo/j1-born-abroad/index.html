    Step by step

    J1

    A Lao baby is born in Thailand

    Somsack gets his birth vaccines in a Thai hospital — and the only thing that will cross the border with him is a pair of paper cards.

    T0 · Birth day · Chiang Rai Provincial Hospital · Hospital records + printed QR cards · told through DA Dr. Anan

    Khamla gives birth to Somsack on 2025-10-28 at Chiang Rai Provincial Hospital, away from home. He has no Thai identity number. Dr Anan gives the Hep B and BCG birth doses and records them with their WHO PreQual product codes. For each dose, the hospital system does three things:

    1. It builds a DVCMinPreQual payload: STT-PAY-001…005 and STT-PAY-007, with subclaim −6 (STT-SEL-003). No identity document is needed (STT-PAY-005).
    2. It prepares the CWT and the bytes to sign: STT-CWT-001…005, STT-ISS-004.
    3. It has XXR's key sign them (STT-COS-002, STT-COS-006), then finishes the HC1 text: STT-COS-001, STT-ZIP-001, STT-B45-001, STT-HC1-001, STT-QR-001.

    Khamla leaves with two paper DVCs. In the demo: the library tests use the WHO guide's own two signed HC1 strings (QR Code Generation §12), which verify against the live DEV trust list under the same XXR key. Somsack's DVC payloads are illustrative, as in VaxLink. Live issuance in the app uses a TEST-ONLY key (STT-ISS-003), whose DSC is not on the GDHCN list, so its DVCs do not verify in J3.

    Before this step (1)
    The issuer's DSC is on the trust list

    Precondition: the issuer's DSC is on the trust list. XXR's document signer certificate (DSC), issued under XXR's Country Signing CA (CSCA), was shared with the GDHCN, the Trust Anchor, before any DVC is signed; the GDHCN publishes it as a DID document. Sharing it is not a step of issuing. A DVC signed with a key whose DSC was never shared fails every receiver's trust-list check (STT-VER-006).

    What if…

    • The cards are lost on the way home. The doses still happened, but no proof travels. Lao clinicians must re-vaccinate or rely on memory.

    Policy point. Babies born abroad can get a verifiable vaccine record before they have the host country’s civil registration or a national ID — and registering the birth at home still matters.

    Under the hood

    Thailand signs the record once. From then on, anyone on the WHO trust network can check it, with no database lookup and no national ID.

    Trust & exchange
    • Digital Vaccine CertificatestandardInternationalDose data packed into CBOR, signed (COSE, ES256) and printed as a QR
      What it is

      A compact vaccination record (CBOR) digitally signed by the issuing country (COSE) and encoded in a QR code. It can be printed on paper.

      So what: Proof travels with the person and can be checked anywhere, even offline and even on paper.

      WHO SMART Trust (HCERT) · HCERT QR code generation

    • WHO GDHCNnetworkInternationalThailand’s signing key (kid 916ffa…) is published on the trust list
      What it is

      A WHO-run network where countries publish the public keys used to sign their health documents, as a shared trust list.

      So what: Lao PDR can verify a Thai signature without a bilateral agreement or a live call to Thailand. Trust is set up once and works between all members.

      WHO GDHCN · WHO SMART Trust

    Requirements exercised

    • STT-PAY-001 Typed DVCMin model
      Statement

      The library SHALL provide a typed DVCMin payload with the short element names and cardinalities of the logical model: n 1..1, dob 1..1, s 1..1, nt 1..1, id 0..1, dt 0..1, gn 0..1, vx 1..1, v 1..1, and a PayloadCodec for it.

    • STT-PAY-002 Typed DVCMinVaccineDetails model
      Statement

      The library SHALL provide the vx structure with vp 1..1, dv 1..1, cn 0..1, is 0..1 and bo 1..1.

    • STT-PAY-003 One immunization event per DVC
      Statement

      A DVCMin SHALL carry exactly one vx. The library SHALL provide a helper that turns one visit with N administered vaccines into N payloads, one per vaccine, and so N certificates.

    • STT-PAY-004 Issuer-or-clinician invariant
      Statement

      Validation SHALL fail a DVCMin whose vx has neither is nor cn (invariant must-have-issuer-or-clinician-name). The issuer SHALL NOT sign a payload that fails validation.

    • STT-PAY-005 Subjects with no identity document
      Statement

      The DVCMin model SHALL allow id and dt to be absent and SHALL support gn (parent or guardian) for subjects such as newborns.

    • STT-PAY-007 Nationality is ISO 3166-1 alpha-3
      Statement

      Validation SHALL check that nt is an ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 code, including user-assigned codes such as XXA, which is used for testing.

    • STT-SEL-003 Development-range subclaims need explicit opt-in
      Statement

      The registry SHALL classify subclaims >= 0 as WHO-reserved and negative subclaims as development range. The issuer SHALL refuse to issue a development-range subclaim unless the issuer policy explicitly allows it. The subclaim number used for a payload type SHALL be configuration, not a constant in a codec, so that DVC can move from -6 to an assigned number without an API break.

    • STT-CWT-001 Integer claim keys
      Statement

      The CWT claim set SHALL use the integer keys 1 (iss), 6 (iat), 4 (exp) and -260 (hcert). The hcert map SHALL use integer subclaim keys. The quoted keys in the documentation ("1", "-260", "-6") are JSON notation only and SHALL NOT be emitted as text keys.

    • STT-CWT-002 NumericDate integers
      Statement

      iat and exp SHALL be encoded as integer NumericDate (seconds since the epoch). The decoder SHOULD accept floating-point NumericDate and truncate it.

    • STT-CWT-003 Expiry is mandatory on issuance
      Statement

      The issuer API SHALL require an explicit validity (exp, or a validity period) and SHALL have no default of "never expires". exp is the only revocation mechanism.

    • STT-CWT-004 Validity inside the DSC validity
      Statement

      Before signing, the issuer SHALL check that iat is not before the DSC notBefore and exp is not after the DSC notAfter, and SHALL refuse to sign otherwise.

    • STT-CWT-005 iss is an optional alpha-2 string
      Statement

      iss SHALL be optional and SHALL be encoded as a text string. When present, the issuer SHALL require two upper-case letters, the alpha-2 form used in iss and in the DSC's X.509 C attribute.

    • STT-ISS-004 Two-phase signing
      Statement

      Issuance SHALL be two-phase: prepare returns the exact bytes to sign (the Sig_structure), the caller signs them by any means, including asynchronous ones such as WebCrypto or a remote KMS, and finish assembles the certificate and returns the HC1 string and the COSE bytes. For ES256, finish SHALL accept r||s or DER and SHALL always emit r||s.

    • STT-COS-002 Both ES256 and PS256
      Statement

      The library SHALL implement signing and verification for ES256 (alg -7, ECDSA P-256 with SHA-256) and PS256 (alg -37, RSASSA-PSS with SHA-256, MGF1 SHA-256, salt length 32, modulus at least 2048 bits).

    • STT-COS-006 No private keys required in-process
      Statement

      The issuer SHALL NOT need access to private key material: issuance is prepare, then the caller signs the returned bytes by any means (HSM, KMS, PKCS#11, Android Keystore, WebCrypto), then finish (STT-ISS-004). A software ES256 and PS256 signer SHALL be provided for tests only, in the testkit module.

    • STT-COS-001 COSE_Sign1 structure
      Statement

      The issuer SHALL emit 18([protected bstr, unprotected map, payload bstr, signature bstr]). The verifier SHALL accept the tagged form and SHOULD also accept an untagged COSE_Sign1 array.

    • STT-ZIP-001 ZLIB/Deflate after signing
      Statement

      The issuer SHALL compress the COSE_Sign1 bytes with ZLIB (RFC 1950) using Deflate (RFC 1951) after signing. The verifier SHALL decompress before parsing COSE.

    • STT-B45-001 Base45 per RFC 9285
      Statement

      Base45 encoding and decoding SHALL follow RFC 9285, the published form of the draft the IG cites.

    • STT-HC1-001 HC1 prefix
      Statement

      The issuer SHALL prefix the Base45 string with "HC1:". The verifier SHALL require a context identifier matching HC[1-9A-Z]: and strip it. It SHALL report any identifier other than HC1 as an unsupported version, distinct from malformed input.

    • STT-QR-001 QR-ready HC1 output; no QR encoding in the library
      Statement

      The HC1 string SHALL consist only of characters in the QR Alphanumeric set (the Base45 alphabet and the "HC1:" prefix), so that integrators can encode it as a single Alphanumeric-mode segment with error correction Q using any QR encoder. The libraries SHALL NOT encode or render QR symbols.

    • STT-ISS-003 Test keys never in production artifacts
      Statement

      Fixed test keys and test PKI (such as the XXA SCA and DSC) SHALL only ship in the testkit module. Production modules SHALL NOT contain or accept them by default.
