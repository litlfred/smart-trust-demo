    Every moment in the journey depends on a few standards and open tools. Pick a big idea or a building block to see which moments it makes possible, or a moment to present it with its technical layer. Specified in The IG · Source.

    Speak the same language

    FHIR and WHO SMART Guidelines mean a dose recorded in Thailand has the same structure and codes in Lao PDR.

    FHIR R4WHO SMART ImmunizationsConceptMapIPS
    Trust without phoning home

    Countries sign records and publish their keys on WHO GDHCN, so anyone can verify a certificate, even offline.

    Digital Vaccine CertificateWHO GDHCNProvenance
    Share without copying

    Data stays in its home country. The family hands out a time-limited key, and every access is logged at home.

    Open tools, offline first

    Thai hospital system + DVC issuing service

    Thailand MOPH · Thailand
    What it is

    The hospital’s own information system records the birth doses and calls a national signing service to print Digital Vaccine Certificates.

    So what for the journey

    Thailand doesn’t need to change its hospital systems. It only needs to be able to sign certificates in the agreed format.
