    Trust, credentials and wallets

    Who signs, who verifies, and who would hold the national trust anchor? Name it; do not decide it.

    Sheet 4: Trust, and who decides (A3)

    The questions, along the journey

    1. J1
      A Lao baby is born in Thailand

      Who in your government would sign this card on behalf of the health sector: the hospital, the ministry, or a national trust service?

    2. J2a
      A health worker finds the baby — and reads his cards

      Who is allowed to verify a foreign credential: a health worker’s phone, a clinic, or only a national service?

    3. J3a
      The clinic confirms the cards are genuine

      Who would hold your national trust anchor, and who accepts a foreign signature on behalf of your health sector?

    4. J3a
      The clinic confirms the cards are genuine

      What must your country have, and what must it decide, before it can verify a neighbour’s card offline? Name it; do not decide it here.

    5. J4.0a
      One phone holds the whole family’s records

      Is a family wallet a national service, a private app, or both — and who decides which apps may hold a record?
