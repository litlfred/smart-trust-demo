    One business process per journey. Each lane is a role on the personas page; each task names the skill that performs it. The BPMN source of each process is in input/business-processes/, and a diagram viewer for each is on the harness site.

    J1 Issue the newborn’s birth-dose DVCs in Country A

    Process id Process_J1BirthDoseDvc. View the diagram.

    J1, Cross-border birth and initial vaccination. In Country A (XXR, the birth country) the provincial hospital gives Chanthala’s Hep B and BCG birth doses and issues one signed DVC per dose (DVCMinPreQual at subclaim -6) as a paper QR: the guide’s QR Code Generation section 12 example. The hospital system is the Issuer (SMART Trust) and the HCERT side of the VHL Sharer; the private key stays with the key holder (two-phase signing, STT-ISS-004, STT-COS-006). XXR’s DSC, under its CSCA, being on the GDHCN trust list is a PRECONDITION of issuing, declared below, not a step. Mali is the Holder (SMART Trust, smart-trust-phw) and VHL Holder (IHE ITI VHL).

    Task checklist: j1-birth-dose-dvc.checklist.md.

    J2 Discover and refer an unregistered infant in Country B

    Process id Process_J2ChwDiscovery. View the diagram.

    J2, Return home, CHW discovery and referral. Back in Country B (home, XXH), where the family resides, the community health worker finds Chanthala unregistered on a home visit. Offline, the CHW app reads the foreign DVCs without verifying them (read-unverified, STT-VER-011: the triage read stops before the trust lookup and marks every field unverified), registers the infant and refers her to the clinic for verification. The CHW app is a Receiver (SMART Trust, smart-trust-phw) that deliberately stops before trust; Mali provides the VDHC as Holder (smart-trust-phw requirement provide-a-vhl-to-a-receiver).

    Task checklist: j2-chw-discovery-referral.checklist.md.

    J3 Verify, import and schedule at the home clinic

    Process id Process_J3ClinicVerification. View the diagram.

    J3, Clinic verification and schedule generation, in Country B. The clinic EHR (Receiver in SMART Trust and smart-trust-phw, VHL Receiver side in IHE ITI VHL) holds a copy of the GDHCN trust list, which the trust anchor (Trust Network Anchor; IHE Trust Anchor) distributes and Country B, a Trust Network Participant, retrieves (smart-trust-phw retrieve-pki-material). Loading the DID documents is a PRECONDITION of this visit, not a step in it: the clinic may be offline throughout, so the list it verifies against is the one it last refreshed, about weekly, when the CHW visits the base clinic or district health office. It verifies each foreign DVC in the STT-VER-001 order against XXR’s DSC DID document, imports the verified doses and evaluates the national schedule, which finds birth-dose OPV due.

    Task checklist: j3-clinic-verification-schedule.checklist.md.

    J4.0 Issue the family’s records to one phone as VHLs

    Process id Process_J40FamilyVhl. View the diagram.

    J4.0, Outbound family travel, “One Family, One Phone”. Before travelling, Mali asks Country B’s national portal for each family member’s records. The portal, a VHL Sharer (IHE ITI VHL) and Issuer (SMART Trust, smart-trust-phw), issues one passcode-protected Verifiable Health Link per member: the documents (IPS, DVCs) encrypted as JWE (STT-VHL-007) behind an ITI-YY5 manifest, the link in the SMART Health Links payload format at HCERT key 5 (STT-VHL-001, STT-VHL-002), signed with Country B’s DSC. The shared phone holds the QRs for Mali, the VHL Holder.

    Task checklist: j4-0-family-vhl-issuance.checklist.md.

    J4.1 Read a family member’s records at the point of care abroad

    Process id Process_J41VhlFetch. View the diagram.

    J4.1. Abroad, Chanthala needs care. Mali, the VHL Holder (IHE ITI VHL; Holder in SMART Trust and smart-trust-phw, requirement provide-a-vhl-to-a-receiver), opens her QR on the family phone and tells the passcode. The hospital system, a VHL Receiver (Receiver in SMART Trust and smart-trust-phw), verifies the HCERT against the GDHCN trust list, requests the manifest by ITI-YY5 with an HTTP Message Signature (STT-VHL-005, STT-VHL-006, STT-VHL-008) and decrypts the documents (STT-VHL-007). If the HCERT does not verify, there is no digital verification: the VHL is not followed and the records are treated like paper. As in J3, loading the DID documents is a PRECONDITION of this visit, not a step in it: the hospital verifies against its cached copy of the trust list and needs no connection to do so. It refreshes that cache nightly or hourly, where an offline site such as the J3 clinic refreshes about weekly.

    Task checklist: j4-1-vhl-point-of-care.checklist.md.
