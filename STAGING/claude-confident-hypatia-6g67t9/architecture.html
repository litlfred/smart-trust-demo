    The journeys and systems of this demonstrator, drawn as an ArchiMate overlay on the DPI-H Reference Architecture’s own ArchiMate model (smart-ra, version 0.3.0-draft.1). The RA’s elements are unchanged and keep their ids, so each RA box here is the element smart-ra publishes. The overlay adds:

    • the journeys J1 to J4.1 (the use cases) as business processes, one per BPMN process;
    • the people who take part, as business roles assigned to the journeys whose BPMN has their lane;
    • each of those roles associated with the RA stakeholder it belongs to: the caregiver with Citizens / Care recipients, the clinicians and the community health worker with Health Workers (a role and a stakeholder are different element types, so ArchiMate allows an association, not a specialization);
    • the Thai and Lao systems, serving the journeys they take part in, each holding its parts;
    • for each country, its own instance of every RA building block its parts play, such as Provider Systems (Thailand) and Provider Systems (Lao PDR): Thailand and Lao PDR are different jurisdictions, so a Thai part is a specialization of Thailand’s instance, which is in turn a specialization of the RA’s block;
    • where the two countries meet, as flow and triggering relationships between their parts, read from the BPMN: the paper DVC that J1 prints and the family carries to J2 and J3; in J4.1, the VHL QR the family’s phone shows the Thai clinic, the clinic’s signed ITI-YY5 manifest request to the Lao gateway, and the manifest it returns, the access logged in Lao PDR;
    • what they share, and nothing else, between them: WHO GDHCN and the WHO SMART Trust specifications it realizes;
    • the RA’s own building blocks, generic and no country’s, in a band of their own below both countries;
    • each journey’s realization of the RA capabilities it exercises.

    Every line is routed orthogonally through the gaps between boxes and never through a box.

    Every box in a drawing links to its element’s page. All views and elements.

    Systems on the Integrated View

    Thailand on the left, Lao PDR on the right, each with its systems above its instances of the RA building blocks (each instance under the part that plays it); between them, only what both share: WHO GDHCN and the SMART Trust specifications. Below both, the RA’s own building blocks, by the layers of its Integrated View (Fig. 3.2). The dashed lines that run from one country to the other are where they meet.

    How it is made

    The model is generated, not drawn: python3 -I tools/archimate/build.py fetches smart-ra’s model and its add_views.py at the commit in tools/archimate/smart-ra.lock.json (sha256 checked), applies tools/archimate/overlay.py, lays the overlay’s views out again with room between the boxes and routes every line orthogonally as Archi bendpoints (tools/archimate/layout.py), and writes archimate/0.1.0/smart-trust-demo.archimate. The journeys and their people are read from processes/content/ and scenarios/roles.json, and where the countries meet from the BPMN’s sequence flows; the systems are those of VaxLink’s architecture view. CI fails if the committed model is not what the build makes. The drawings are cat-harness’s archimate subgraph’s: no Archi, no Java.
