    J2a

    A health worker finds the baby — and reads his cards

    On a routine village visit, Vilay finds a newborn the Lao system doesn’t know exists. She can read the vaccines on his cards, but she can’t prove they are genuine.

    T0 + 3 weeks · Catchment Village 04 · Offline phone app (QR scan) · told through VI Vilay

    On a routine visit to Village 04, Vilay finds a newborn the Lao system does not know exists. Her phone has had no signal for weeks. Khamla shows the two cards. The CHW app reads them with read-unverified (STT-VER-011; STT-B45-002, STT-ZIP-002, STT-CBR-003), which needs no trust list. It shows the doses, but checks no signature and saves nothing yet.

    What if…

    • What if the CHW phone could verify signatures offline too? Technically possible with a cached trust list — but who keeps it current on thousands of village phones?

    Policy point. Keep a clear line between what families report and what has been verified, and don’t grant trust too early.

    Under the hood

    The phone can read the QR but deliberately doesn’t trust it: it decodes the card offline and checks no signature. Nothing is written yet.

    Building blocks used

    Tools & apps
    • OHS FHIR SDKtoolInternationalDecodes the QR offline
      What it is

      An open-source, multiplatform library for building apps that store FHIR data on the device, work fully offline and sync later.

      So what: The CHW phone, clinic tablet and family wallet are all built on the same open building block, so data is FHIR from the first tap. No conversion step, no vendor lock-in.

      OHS Android FHIR SDK

    Trust & exchange
    • Digital Vaccine CertificatestandardInternationalQR decoded (CBOR) but signature not checked
      What it is

      A compact vaccination record (CBOR) digitally signed by the issuing country (COSE) and encoded in a QR code. It can be printed on paper.

      So what: Proof travels with the person and can be checked anywhere, even offline and even on paper.

      WHO SMART Trust (HCERT) · HCERT QR code generation

    Data standards

      What it is

      WHO’s machine-readable immunization guidance: data dictionary, FHIR profiles, PreQual vaccine product codes and schedule logic.

      So what: Both countries use the same WHO vaccine codes and profiles, so a dose recorded in Thailand means the same thing in Lao PDR.

      WHO SMART Immunizations

    Requirements exercised

    • STT-VER-011 Unverified read for triage
      Statement

      The verifier SHALL offer an explicitly named unverified read (read-unverified) that decodes an HC1 string up to and including the payload without any trust lookup or signature check, for display and triage where verification is not possible, such as a community health worker offline at a household (journey J2). Its result type SHALL be distinct from a verified result, SHALL carry the kid, the claims and every hcert entry it found, and SHALL state that nothing in it is verified. It SHALL apply the same decode limits as verify.

    • STT-B45-002 Strict Base45 decoding
      Statement

      The decoder SHALL reject characters outside the 45-character alphabet, a dangling single character, and triplets or pairs whose value overflows (above 65535, or above 255 for a trailing pair).

    • STT-ZIP-002 Decompression bomb protection
      Statement

      Inflation SHALL stop with a typed error once the output exceeds a configurable limit (DecodeLimits.maxInflatedBytes).

    • STT-CBR-003 Bounded CBOR decoding
      Statement

      The CBOR decoder SHALL enforce configurable limits on nesting depth, item count and string/byte-string length, and SHALL reject malformed or truncated input with a typed error, without panicking or throwing unchecked exceptions.

    Registered at home, and referred

    With no signal, Vilay adds Somsack to his household, saves his doses as “reported”, and refers the family to the clinic.

    T0 + 3 weeks · Catchment Village 04 · Offline phone app (household form + referral) · told through VI Vilay

    On the CHW app's newborn form (structured data capture, TrustDemoHouseholdRegistration) she adds Somsack to the Sengmany household (TrustDemoHousehold), with a temporary id. Bounmy confirms the details as head of household. The app saves the doses read from the cards as reported by the family, not verified (TrustDemoJ2HepBReported, TrustDemoJ2BcgReported: primarySource false). Vilay refers Somsack to the district health center (TrustDemoJ2Referral). Everything stays queued on the phone, for 28 days if need be, and syncs when it next reaches a signal.

    What if…

    • Vilay’s phone stays offline for 28 days. Everything stays queued safely on the device and syncs when she next reaches the health center.

    Policy point. Newborns can be registered at home, offline, before civil registration or a national ID exists.

    Under the hood

    The registration form is a FHIR Questionnaire. Its answers become standard FHIR resources on the phone; the doses read from the cards are saved with primarySource=false, apart from verified data, and everything syncs whenever there is signal.

    Building blocks used

    Tools & apps
    • OHS FHIR SDKtoolInternationalStores data on the phone; works 28+ days offline
      What it is

      An open-source, multiplatform library for building apps that store FHIR data on the device, work fully offline and sync later.

      So what: The CHW phone, clinic tablet and family wallet are all built on the same open building block, so data is FHIR from the first tap. No conversion step, no vendor lock-in.

      OHS Android FHIR SDK

    • SDC librarytoolInternationalRenders the household registration form and extracts FHIR from the answers
      What it is

      Renders FHIR Questionnaires as forms on the device and turns the answers into FHIR resources (extraction).

      So what: The Ministry can change a registration form by publishing a new Questionnaire, without rebuilding the app.

      HL7 FHIR SDC · FHIR Questionnaire · This journey’s form (IG)

    • Info Gateway (AuthZ)toolInternationalOn sync, checks the CHW’s token and only lets her write to households in her own catchment
      What it is

      The authorization (AuthZ) layer in front of the national FHIR server. It checks the caller’s access token from the identity provider, applies access rules (role, facility, household, consent) to every request, and records each access.

      So what: Every app, from the CHW phone to the Thai clinic viewer, goes through one front door. Lao PDR keeps the records at home and decides who can read or write what, including requests from abroad.

      OHS FHIR Info Gateway

    Data standards
    • FHIR R4standardInternationalPatient and Group (household); Immunization.primarySource = false; ServiceRequest for referral
      What it is

      The global standard for representing and exchanging health data as resources (Patient, Immunization, …) over a web API.

      So what: It is the common language. Every system in this story reads and writes the same resource shapes.

      HL7 FHIR R4 · This journey as a FHIR IG
