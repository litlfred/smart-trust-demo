    The indicators are the DAK’s component 7: what a programme would count from the data the journeys record, as the WHO SMART Guidelines DAK defines them. Each gives its numerator and denominator in words and as a computation over the data elements, and the disaggregations it is reported by. Each is also a FHIR Measure (proportion scoring) whose populations are described, not computable: this guide carries no CQL.

    These are definitions only. They state no target, no threshold and no value: the journeys are a demonstration with fictional people, and nothing here has been measured.

    The source is dak/indicators.json; this page and the Measures are generated from it.

    Indicator Definition Journeys Measure
    TD.IND.01 Foreign doses imported as verified Of the foreign DVCs a clinic scans to verify, the proportion whose dose is recorded as verified. J3 TrustDemoIND01
    TD.IND.02 Shared-link openings that were authorised Of the requests to open a family member’s shared link, the proportion the home country released. J4.1 TrustDemoIND02
    TD.IND.03 CHW referrals for verification completed at the clinic Of the referrals community health workers make for a child’s foreign cards to be verified, the proportion completed at the clinic. J2, J3 TrustDemoIND03
    TD.IND.04 Imported doses carrying a national schedule code Of the foreign doses imported as verified, the proportion that carry a national schedule code beside the issuer’s code, and so count toward the schedule. J3 TrustDemoIND04

    TD.IND.01 Foreign doses imported as verified

    Of the foreign DVCs a clinic scans to verify, the proportion whose dose is recorded as verified.

       
    Numerator Foreign DVCs scanned for verification in the reporting period whose dose was recorded as verified.
    Numerator computation COUNT of verification records (TD.J3A) WHERE “Verified at” is in the reporting period AND “Record outcome” = verified
    Denominator Foreign DVCs scanned for verification in the reporting period.
    Denominator computation COUNT of verification records (TD.J3A) WHERE “Verified at” is in the reporting period
    Disaggregations Record outcome: verified, reported-unverified, rejected, not-read (TD.DT.01)
    Failure reason: the STT-VER-008 reason, for DVCs that did not verify
    Issuing country: the participant that signed the DVC
    Facility: the clinic that verified
    Data elements TD.J3A.DE02 Issuing country
    TD.J3A.DE03 Verified at
    TD.J3A.DE05 Verification result
    TD.J3A.DE07 Failure reason
    TD.J3A.DE09 Record outcome
    Decision tables TD.DT.01
    Requirements STT-VER-001, STT-VER-006, STT-VER-008
    Journeys J3
    Measure TrustDemoIND01

    TD.IND.02 Shared-link openings that were authorised

    Of the requests to open a family member’s shared link, the proportion the home country released.

       
    Numerator Manifest requests in the reporting period that were released.
    Numerator computation COUNT of access records (TD.J41) WHERE “Requested at” is in the reporting period AND “Released” = true
    Denominator Manifest requests in the reporting period, released or refused.
    Denominator computation COUNT of access records (TD.J41) WHERE “Requested at” is in the reporting period
    Disaggregations Refusal reason: wrong-passcode, expired, withdrawn (TD.DT.05)
    Requesting system: the receiver abroad, and its country
    Data elements TD.J41.DE01 Requested at
    TD.J41.DE02 Requester
    TD.J41.DE06 Released
    TD.J41.DE08 Refusal reason
    Decision tables TD.DT.05
    Requirements STD-ACC-001
    Journeys J4.1
    Measure TrustDemoIND02 (basis AuditEvent)

    TD.IND.03 CHW referrals for verification completed at the clinic

    Of the referrals community health workers make for a child’s foreign cards to be verified, the proportion completed at the clinic.

       
    Numerator Referrals made in the reporting period for which the clinic has recorded an outcome for every dose the referral lists.
    Numerator computation COUNT of referrals (TD.J2B) WHERE “Referred at” is in the reporting period AND “Referral status” = completed
    Denominator Referrals for verification made by community health workers in the reporting period.
    Denominator computation COUNT of referrals (TD.J2B) WHERE “Referred at” is in the reporting period
    Disaggregations Referred to: the clinic referred to
    Referred by: the community health worker
    Data elements TD.J2B.DE02 Referred by
    TD.J2B.DE03 Referred to
    TD.J2B.DE04 Referred at
    TD.J2B.DE06 Doses to verify
    TD.J2B.DE07 Referral status
    Decision tables TD.DT.01
    Requirements STT-VER-011
    Journeys J2, J3
    Measure TrustDemoIND03 (basis ServiceRequest)

    TD.IND.04 Imported doses carrying a national schedule code

    Of the foreign doses imported as verified, the proportion that carry a national schedule code beside the issuer’s code, and so count toward the schedule.

       
    Numerator Doses imported as verified in the reporting period that carry a national schedule code.
    Numerator computation COUNT of imported doses (TD.J3B) WHERE imported in the reporting period AND “Mapping basis” in (concept-map, clinician-confirmed)
    Denominator Doses imported as verified in the reporting period.
    Denominator computation COUNT of imported doses (TD.J3B) WHERE imported in the reporting period AND “Mapping basis” != not-imported
    Disaggregations Mapping basis: concept-map, clinician-confirmed, unmapped (TD.DT.02)
    Issuer’s product code: the product as signed
    Data elements TD.J3B.DE01 Issuer’s product code
    TD.J3B.DE02 National schedule code
    TD.J3B.DE03 Mapping basis
    TD.J3B.DE05 Counts toward the schedule
    Decision tables TD.DT.02, TD.DT.03
    Requirements STD-SEM-001, STD-SCH-001
    Journeys J3
    Measure TrustDemoIND04 (basis Immunization)
