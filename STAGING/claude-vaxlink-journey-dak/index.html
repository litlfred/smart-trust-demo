One baby · two countries · one year

Somsack is born in Thailand and grows up in Lao PDR. A year later, a Thai doctor sees every vaccine he has ever had, and no record is copied across the border.

This guide is that story, the VaxLink walkthrough, written down as a WHO SMART Guidelines digital adaptation kit (L2) and a FHIR implementation guide (L3). It is built on the SMART Trust HCERT libraries and WHO GDHCN.

Four moments that matter

Each moment is one business process (BPMN), played by fictional people. Each one is linked to the user scenario, the process and the FHIR records it creates.

1. Born abroad
Birth day · Chiang Rai, Thailand · J1

A Lao baby gets his first vaccines in Thailand. The hospital signs each dose as a Digital Vaccination Certificate, and the family leaves with two paper QR cards.

Scenario · Process · Thai profile
2. Found at home
3 weeks later · Village 04, Lao PDR · J2

A health worker registers him offline and reads the cards. She records them as "reported, not yet verified" and refers him to the clinic.

Scenario · Process · Lao profile
3. Made official
4 weeks later · district health centre, Lao PDR · J3

A nurse checks the signatures against the GDHCN trust list, offline. She maps the Thai vaccine codes to the Lao schedule and books the 6-week doses.

Scenario · Process · Lao profile
4. Crossing back
12 months later · Lao PDR → Thailand · J4.0, J4.1

The family shares his whole record with a Thai doctor through a Verifiable Health Link on Dad's phone. Every access is logged back home.

Scenario · Process · Lao profile

How trust works here

Thailand and Lao PDR never connect to each other. Both build on WHO GDHCN. Thailand publishes the key it signs certificates with, and Lao PDR caches the trust list, so its clinics can verify a Thai card with no signal. A year later the same trust list lets a Thai clinic verify the Lao VHL. Data crosses the border in only two ways: as a signed QR card the family carries, or as a link the family chooses to share. The VaxLink walkthrough’s “How it works” view lays out both countries’ components on the DPI-H reference architecture, with GDHCN as the foundation.

The journeys run the HCERT and VHL libraries of smart-trust-tools against WHO SMART Trust and WHO SMART Trust PHW.
