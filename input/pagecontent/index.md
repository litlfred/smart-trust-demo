This guide is the WHO SMART Guidelines L2 digital adaptation kit (DAK) and L3 implementation guide of the SMART Trust demonstrator. It follows one family across borders through four journeys:

| | Journey | Where |
|---|---|---|
| J1 | Issue the newborn's birth-dose DVCs | Country A (XXR) |
| J2 | Discover and refer an unregistered infant | Country B (home) |
| J3 | Verify, import and schedule at the home clinic | Country B (home) |
| J4.0 | Issue the family's records to one phone as VHLs | Country B (home) |
| J4.1 | Read a family member's records at the point of care abroad | abroad |
{: .grid }

The journeys exercise the HCERT and VHL libraries of [smart-trust-tools](https://github.com/litlfred/smart-trust-tools) against [WHO SMART Trust](https://smart.who.int/trust) and [WHO SMART Trust PHW](https://smart.who.int/trust-phw). Every person in them is fictional.

- [Generic personas](personas.html) and [system actors](system-actors.html): the roles that play each process lane.
- [User scenarios](scenarios.html): the user stories and the journeys step by step.
- [Business processes](business-processes.html): one BPMN process per journey.
- [Functional requirements](functional-requirements.html): where the journeys' requirements are defined.

The project: the [journeys J1 to J4](journeys.html) in full, the [plan](plan.html), the [app-platform proposal](proposal-app-platform.html) and the [dependency register](dependencies.html).

The source is [litlfred/smart-trust-demo](https://github.com/litlfred/smart-trust-demo). This guide is a demonstrator, not WHO guidance.
