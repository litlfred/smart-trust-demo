    The story in one sentence

    Somsak is born in Taifafeki, grows up in Onesa, and one year later a Taifafeki doctor can see every vaccine he has ever had — without a single record being copied across the border.

    A baby and five people around him, four moments, twelve months. Follow the people first; the technology is what makes each hand-off possible.

    Four moments that matter

    1Taifafeki Born abroad birth day · Taifafeki An Onesan baby gets his first vaccines in Taifafeki and leaves with signed paper cards. KSDA 2Onesa Found at home 3 weeks later · Onesa A health worker registers him offline and records the cards as "reported, not yet verified". KSBSVI 3Onesa Made official 4 weeks later · Lomaru District Health Center A nurse verifies the cards are genuine and fits the Taifafeki doses into the Onesan schedule. KSCH 4Cross-Border Crossing back 6 to 12 months later · Onesa and Kesaro The family's records go on one shared phone as Verifiable Health Links (J4.0), and a year later a doctor back in Taifafeki reads the whole history from Onesa (J4.1). One chapter of the walkthrough, two BPMN processes, so two sub-journeys. KSBSDA
