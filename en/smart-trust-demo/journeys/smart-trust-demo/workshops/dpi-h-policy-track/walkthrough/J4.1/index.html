    Policy takeaway

    Continuity of care across borders: each country keeps its own records, with no international patient registry and no vendor lock-in. What is agreed regionally is the data model and the trust list, not a shared copy of the data.

    Policy questions for the table

    1. M5What agreement makes every view abroad leave an entry in the home country’s log — and who reads it?
    2. M6What would your country send to a regional connectathon within twelve months to prove this moment works?
    3. M7What is a realistic twelve-month first move toward this moment, with a named owner — and which cost driver dominates it?
