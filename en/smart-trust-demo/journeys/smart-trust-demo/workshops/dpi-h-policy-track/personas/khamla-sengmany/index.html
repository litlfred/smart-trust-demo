    Khamla Mother · Onesa

    Goal: Have Somsak's care recognized at home, without starting over.

    Needs
    • Proof of the vaccines her baby got abroad
    • Clear guidance on when to come back
    • Not to be penalized for giving birth across the border
    Frustrations
    • Paper cards are easy to lose
    • Extra trips to the clinic
    • The family's records live on her husband's phone

    How Khamla feels along the way

    A dashed line: not present in between · a point opens that moment

    Khamla’s touchpoints (7)

    1. J1 T0 · Birth day · Kesaro Provincial Hospital

      An Onesan baby is born in Taifafeki

      Gives birth away from home and receives two paper cards at discharge.

      “Will anyone back home believe these papers?”

      Touchpoint: Hospital records + printed QR cards
      Worried Present
    2. J2a T0 + 3 weeks · Catchment Village 04

      A health worker finds the baby — and reads his cards

      Tells Vilay about the birth in Taifafeki and shows the cards.

      “I hope we’re not in trouble for giving birth abroad.”

      Touchpoint: Offline phone app (QR scan)
      Worried Present
    3. J2b T0 + 3 weeks · Catchment Village 04

      Registered at home, and referred

      Receives a referral to the District Health Center.

      “Another trip — but at least they know about his vaccines now.”

      Touchpoint: Offline phone app (household form + referral)
      Neutral Present
    4. J3a T0 + 4 weeks · Lomaru District Health Center

      The clinic confirms the cards are genuine

      Brings Somsak in for the referral visit.

      “They believe the Taifafeki papers!”

      Touchpoint: Clinic tablet (verification)
      Reassured Present
    5. J3b T0 + 4 weeks · Lomaru District Health Center

      Taifafeki vaccines are fitted to the Onesan schedule

      Leaves with a date for Somsak’s next vaccines.

      “Now I know exactly when to come back.”

      Touchpoint: Clinic tablet (vaccine matching + schedule)
      Delighted Present
    6. J4.0a T0 + 12 months · Lomaru, Onesa

      One phone holds the whole family’s records

      Relies on Bounmy’s phone for her own and the baby’s records.

      “It’s on his phone. What if I travel without him?”

      Touchpoint: Family wallet app on Dad’s phone
      Worried Present
    7. J4.1 T0 + 12 months · Kesaro primary care clinic

      A Taifafeki doctor sees the full history

      Watches the doctor see everything that happened since the birth.

      “It all followed him.”

      Touchpoint: Clinic viewer (scan link + passcode)
      Delighted Present
