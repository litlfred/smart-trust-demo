    Dr. Anan Taifafeki clinician · birth & follow-up · Taifafeki

    Goal: Give a non-Taifafeki baby proper care, at birth and when he returns.

    Needs
    • A way to issue records that travel with the family
    • A child's full history at the point of care
    • Confidence the data is authentic
    Frustrations
    • No national ID to attach records to
    • Records from abroad he cannot read or verify

    How Dr. Anan feels along the way

    A dashed line: not present in between · a point opens that moment

    Dr. Anan’s touchpoints (2)

    1. J1 T0 · Birth day · Kesaro Provincial Hospital

      An Onesan baby is born in Taifafeki

      Gives Hep B and BCG birth doses and prints two signed QR vaccine cards.

      “He isn’t Taifafeki, but he still needs a record he can take home.”

      Touchpoint: Hospital records + printed QR cards
      Reassured Present
    2. J4.1 T0 + 12 months · Kesaro primary care clinic

      A Taifafeki doctor sees the full history

      Scans the QR, enters the passcode Bounmy tells him, and sees all six doses.

      “Born here, vaccinated there — and I can see all of it.”

      Touchpoint: Clinic viewer (scan link + passcode)
      Delighted Present
