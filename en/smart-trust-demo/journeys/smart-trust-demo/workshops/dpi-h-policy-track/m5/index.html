    Module M5 · day 2 · Verifiable immunization records across borders: the policy-maker track

    Governance, decision rights and the legal basis

    Who holds each decision — and what legal basis lets the record cross a border and be shared for a child?

    By the end, a participant can

    • Name who holds each of the four decision rights today
    • Name the legal basis that lets a record cross a border and be shared for a child

    Sheet 4: Trust, and who decides (A3)

    The questions, along the journey

    1. J1
      An Onesan baby is born in Taifafeki

      Does your law let a facility issue a health credential to a child who is not a citizen and has no national ID?

    2. J2b
      Registered at home, and referred

      Who may create a health identity for a newborn at a home visit, and on whose consent?

    3. J4.0a
      One phone holds the whole family’s records

      Who may hold and share a child’s record: either parent, a guardian, the child from what age? What does your law say about dependants?

    4. J4.0b
      Dad creates a link to share at the clinic

      What legal basis lets a record held at home be opened by a clinician abroad, on the family’s consent?

    5. J4.0b
      Dad creates a link to share at the clinic

      Would one clause in your procurement — conformance to the national IG — be enough for your portal to issue this link?

    6. J4.0b
      Dad creates a link to share at the clinic

      Who sets how a shared link is protected — the passcode, how long it lasts, who may open it: a national rule, or the family’s choice?

    7. J4.1
      A Taifafeki doctor sees the full history

      What agreement makes every view abroad leave an entry in the home country’s log — and who reads it?
