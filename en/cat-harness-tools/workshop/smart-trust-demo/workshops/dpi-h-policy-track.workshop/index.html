    Verifiable immunization records across borders: the policy-maker track
    description
    The seven modules (M1 to M7) of the DPI-H workshop's policy-maker track, taught from the journeys J1 to J4.1 of one cross-border family: domestic and cross-border verifiable immunization records as the entry point to a personal health record.
    audience
    Ministry of Health policy makers and digital health leads, at tables of one country each
    promise
    You will not leave with an assessment or a plan. You will leave knowing what each consists of, where your country stands on its first page, and who you would do the rest with.
    useCaseStatement
    A child vaccinated in ___ should be able to ___ in ___.
