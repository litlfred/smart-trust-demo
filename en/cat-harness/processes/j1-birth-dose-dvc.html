J1 Issue the newborn’s birth-dose DVCs in Country A

Process_J1BirthDoseDvc · advisory · 6 step(s)

J1, Cross-border birth and initial vaccination. In Country A (XXR, the birth country) the provincial hospital gives a newborn who is not its own country’s national the Hep B and BCG birth doses and issues one signed DVC per dose (DVCMinPreQual at subclaim -6) as a paper QR, with no national identity number needed (the WHO guide’s QR Code Generation section 12 example is such a pair). The hospital system is the Issuer (SMART Trust) and the HCERT side of the VHL Sharer; the private key stays with the key holder (two-phase signing, STT-ISS-004, STT-COS-006). XXR’s DSC, under its CSCA, being on the GDHCN trust list is a PRECONDITION of issuing, declared below, not a step. The caregiver is the Holder (SMART Trust, smart-trust-phw) and VHL Holder (IHE ITI VHL).

step lane skill / sub-process what it does
Give Hep B and BCG birth doses and record them
Task_Vaccinate		 Birth clinic vaccinator calls Process_QuestionnaireDataCapture The vaccinator records each dose (vaccine product, date, batch) in the hospital system, on its dose form, through smart-base’s reusable SDC data-capture process: complete-sdc-questionnaire, then extract-questionnaire-response, which writes the Immunization each DVC is built from.
Build one DVCMinPreQual payload per dose
Task_Payload		 Birth clinic issuing system hcert-select-payload One immunization event per DVC (STT-PAY-003), typed DVCMin models (STT-PAY-001, STT-PAY-002), nationality alpha-3 XXK (STT-PAY-007), subject with no identity document (STT-PAY-005); subclaim -6 in the development range, opted into explicitly (STT-SEL-003).
Prepare: CWT claims and the COSE Sig_structure
Task_Prepare		 Birth clinic issuing system hcert-build-cwt iss XR, iat, exp inside the DSC validity (STT-CWT-001 to 005); kid from the DSC (STT-KID-001); the library returns the bytes to sign (STT-ISS-004).
Sign the bytes with XXR’s DSC key
Task_Sign		 Issuer signing key hcert-sign-cose ES256 or PS256 (STT-COS-002) in the HSM, KMS or Keystore; the library never holds the private key (STT-COS-006).
Finish: COSE_Sign1, deflate, Base45, HC1:
