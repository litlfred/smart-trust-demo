[A digital representation of a data set comprising …] BT: A digital representation of a dataset consisting of a certificate or document, designed for a set of specific clinical use cases or public health use cases, and defined using open and interoperable digital health standards; it includes, or is linked to, a digital signature that can be verified by the public key of a public-private cryptographic key pair, and it is issued by a GDHCN participant.
2. [A formal application sent via a verifiable and sec…] BT: A formal request sent through a secure and verifiable channel from an eligible GDHCN participant to the GDHCN Secretariat to join the GDHCN trust network and to participate in a given trust domain.
3. [A service (digital or otherwise) related to the is…] BT: A service (digital or non-digital) relating to the issuance, management, verification or exchange of verifiable digital health certificates, or other related operations, defined using open and interoperable digital health standards.
4. [A system of hardware, software, policies, procedur…] BT: A system of hardware, software, policies, procedures and roles that supports the management of public keys and supports authentication, encryption, integrity or non-repudiation services through a database of digital public keys.
5. [A Trust Network is a means to authenticate the enc…] BT: A trust network is a means of authenticating the public cryptographic keys used by participants within a network to perform cryptographic services, verify digital signatures, establish secure communications between systems, and otherwise make use of public cryptographic keys.
6. [An Eligible GDHCN Participant is one of the follow…] BT: An eligible GDHCN participant is one of the following: • a Member State or Associate Member of the World Health Organization (WHO), or a subnational unit thereof; • a State Party to the International Health Regulations (2005), or a subnational unit thereof; • the United Nations (UN) and other intergovernmental organizations that have effective relations with the World Health Organization (WHO); • a fund, programme, specialized agency or related organization within the United Nations (UN) system; or • an organization formally mandated by one of the organizations mentioned above, able to commit to the terms of participation in GDHCN, and performing one or more of the following health service functions: • public health authority; • health professions education accreditation authority; • health services licensing authority; or • public health security authority. Once accepted into GDHCN, after successful completion of the onboarding process, it is referred to throughout this document as a GDHCN participant. An eligible GDHCN participant that has started the onboarding process is referred to throughout this document as an eligible GDHCN applicant.
7. [An entity that stores, signs, and issues cryptogra…] BT: An entity that stores, signs and issues cryptographic certificates, and certifies ownership of the public key by the certificate holder named in it. This allows participants in the GDHCN trust network gateway to rely on signatures or assertions issued with respect to the private key corresponding to the certified public key. Certification authorities may be operated by different types of entities, including but not limited to: a private-sector certification authority, a ministry or agency responsible for information and communication technology (ICT) services or e-government, a ministry of health, or another competent authority within the jurisdiction. Each GDHCN participant is responsible for determining the appropriate arrangement for its certification authority, in accordance with the laws and policies applicable within its jurisdiction, so as to ensure security and proper data management. Each GDHCN participant must identify, establish or coordinate with the relevant certification authority, as appropriate, and impose on it all the requirements of this document.

[An organization or organizational unit that is res…] BT: An organization or organizational unit responsible for establishing and/or implementing licensing procedures, and for licensing organizations or individuals to provide clinical services, health system services or public health services within its jurisdiction.
