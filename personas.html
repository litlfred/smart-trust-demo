    These are the generic personas of the journeys J1 to J4: the roles that play the lanes of the business processes, each defined as an ActorDefinition. The people and systems that play them in the journeys are fictional.

    Generic personas

    Role Description Played by (fictional) Also known as
    Caregiver The parent who holds the child’s paper DVCs and the family phone, and presents them; IHE ITI VHL VHL Holder and smart-trust Holder. Bounmy SENGMANY (father, holds the family phone); Khamla SENGMANY (mother) VHL Holder (IHE ITI VHL); Holder (SMART Trust)
    Birth clinic vaccinator The clinician abroad who gives the birth doses and hands over the signed paper DVC (J1). Dr Anan (clinician, Chiang Rai, Thailand)  
    Community health worker Finds unregistered children on home visits, registers them and refers them to the clinic (J2). Vilay (community health worker, Village 04, Lao PDR)  
    Home clinic provider The nurse at the home clinic who receives the referral, has the foreign DVCs verified and decides catch-up doses (J3). Nurse Chanthasone (Vientiane District Health Center, Lao PDR)  
    Point-of-care provider The clinician abroad who scans the family’s QR and reads the child’s records (J4.1). Dr Anan (clinician, Chiang Rai, Thailand)  

    System actors

    Role Description Played by (fictional) Also known as
    Birth clinic issuing system The hospital system abroad that issues a DVC per dose with the HCERT library: the HCERT issuing system (smart-trust-tools) of a trust network participant (XXR). Chiang Rai Provincial Hospital system, Thailand (Country A, XXR) Issuer of the birth-dose DVCs (SMART Trust Issuer)
    CHW app The OHS app on the CHW’s phone: reads a foreign DVC without verifying it (triage read, STT-VER-011), registers the child and records a referral, all offline, syncing later. CHW app, Lao PDR (Country B) Triage reader (SMART Trust Receiver, read-unverified)
    Home clinic EHR The clinic’s OHS-based record system: verifies DVCs against the GDHCN trust list, imports them as FHIR Immunization resources and evaluates the national schedule (J3). Vientiane District Health Center EHR (Country B) Verifier at the home clinic (SMART Trust Receiver)
    National health portal The home country’s portal that issues each family member’s records (IPS, DVCs) as a passcode-protected Verifiable Health Link and hosts the manifests (J4.0), checking the passcode, expiry and consent on each request and logging every access at home (J4.1): the VHL Sharer. Lao PDR national health portal and gateway (Country B, XXH) VHL Sharer (IHE ITI VHL); Issuer of the family’s VHLs (SMART Trust Issuer)
    Family phone The one smartphone the family shares, holding one VHL QR per member (J4); holds links, not keys to anyone’s records beyond what each link grants. The family’s shared phone Personal health wallet
    Point-of-care receiver The clinic system abroad that scans a VHL, verifies it, fetches the manifest (ITI-YY5) and decrypts the documents (J4.1): the VHL Receiver. Chiang Rai primary care clinic viewer, Thailand (J4.1) VHL Receiver (IHE ITI VHL); Receiver of the family’s VHLs (SMART Trust Receiver)

    External actors

    Role Description Played by (fictional) Also known as

    GDHCN trust anchor WHO’s GDHCN, which receives participants’ PKI material and distributes it as DID trust lists; Trust Network Anchor (SMART Trust), Trust Anchor (IHE ITI VHL). GDHCN DEV trust network (WHO) Trust Network Anchor (SMART Trust); Trust Anchor (IHE ITI VHL)
